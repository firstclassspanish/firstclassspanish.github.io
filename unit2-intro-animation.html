Spanish greetings follow the clock.

In Spanish, greetings are simple — you just need to know what time of day it is.

HolaBuenos díasBuenas tardesBuenas noches

Hola works anytime. But most saludos (greetings) match the time of day — morning, afternoon, or night.

AdiósHasta luegoHasta mañanaNos vemos

Saying goodbye works the same way — a handful of simple, reusable despedidas (goodbyes).
